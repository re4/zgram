05 // KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Questions,
answered.

01 Is Zgram the official Telegram app?

No. Zgram is an independent, community-built modification of Telegram Desktop. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Telegram Messenger LLP.

02 Where should I download Zgram?

Use the download posts in the official updates channel at t.me/zgram_io. That is where current builds and release notes are published.

03 What data does the Local Archive keep?

Only chats you explicitly enable are archived. The archive can retain locally observed messages, edits, and deletion context. Secret, self-destructing, view-once, and protected content is excluded.

04 Can I inspect the source?

Yes. Zgram is based on the open-source Telegram Desktop codebase, and the modified source is available on GitHub.
